Case study / Enterprise search

Making enterprise search faster, smarter, and more actionable.

Leverage AI/ML to improve search relevancy and guide users to the tools and services they need in the least amount of time.

In this case study

The challenge

Employees were searching across disconnected systems with inconsistent results, unclear terminology and no reliable way to understand which answer or service was right for them.

The opportunity was to make search feel less like a directory and more like a useful front door to the organisation.

My role

I led the product design direction for the search experience, bringing together user needs, content strategy, AI/ML opportunities and the technical constraints of a complex enterprise platform.

I worked with product, engineering and business stakeholders to establish a clearer search vision and a practical path toward delivery.

Designing for relevance

We shaped the experience around the different ways people search for help, information and colleagues.

  • A clearer, more focused search entry point
  • More useful result grouping and visual hierarchy
  • Improved relevance through AI/ML-assisted discovery
  • People search that makes expertise easier to find
  • A foundation that can evolve with the wider employee experience

The result was a more direct path from a question to a useful answer, service or person.

Impact

The work established a more coherent direction for enterprise search and created a stronger foundation for improving relevance, findability and trust across the employee experience.

Leadership takeaway: Search became a strategic product experience rather than a standalone utility. The work aligned design, product and engineering around a clearer vision for helping employees find what they need with less friction.
