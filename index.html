02 / Approach

Designing clarity from complexity.

I help organisations make complex systems, services and decisions feel clearer, simpler and more useful.

My work sits at the intersection of experience strategy, product thinking and design craft. I use discovery, research, analytics and service design to understand what is really getting in people’s way—then bring business, product, engineering and design teams together around a practical path forward.

I’m particularly experienced in digital workplace, employee experience and business intelligence products, where success depends on more than a good interface. It means aligning different priorities, navigating technical and organisational constraints, and creating experiences that earn adoption through genuine usefulness.

I bring a calm, collaborative approach to high-pressure environments, working closely with senior stakeholders to turn ambiguity into shared direction and measurable progress.

03 / Career

A career shaped
by design.

I’ve spent more than 30 years designing how people experience brands, products and services.

My career began in visual communication and brand design, then evolved through digital, UX and product design as technology changed the way organisations work and connect with people. That breadth still shapes my perspective today: I care about the craft of the interface, but also the strategy, systems and collaboration needed to make a meaningful difference.

I’ve worked in agencies, consultancies and large enterprises, across financial services, technology, media, public sector and consumer brands. From early digital experiences to complex employee platforms, the constant has been a belief that good design makes life feel clearer, easier and more considered.

Today, I focus on leading experience strategy for complex products—helping organisations turn competing needs and difficult systems into services people can understand, trust and use with confidence.
