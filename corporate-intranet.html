Case study / Corporate intranet

Transforming the employee experience through a new corporate intranet.

Creating an intuitive experience that helps employees stay informed, collaborate and quickly access timely, personalised resources.

Role
Product design lead
Scope
Design leadership · Product strategy
Launch
July 2023
Status

In this case study

The mandate

The firm’s intranet was archaic, fragmented and difficult to maintain. Employees struggled to find the resources and information they needed, while teams across the organisation were publishing content through inconsistent, disconnected experiences.

I was asked to lead the product design and launch of a new enterprise intranet, establishing a more intuitive employee experience and bringing a coherent product vision to a complex organisation. The initial launch was delivered in approximately 12 months.

My role

I set the experience vision and design direction for the programme, building the design and research capability required to deliver it.

I worked with senior leaders across the lines of business, Corporate Communications, Marketing and Brand to align competing organisational priorities around a shared employee experience. My role was to maintain a clear focus on employee needs while creating a platform that could serve the distinct requirements of the wider firm.

The product transformation

We redesigned the intranet around employees’ everyday needs rather than the firm’s internal structure.

  1. A new information architecture that made resources easier to find
  2. A personalised, customisable homepage
  3. Streamlined access to learning, career information, requests, approvals and tasks
  4. A guided experience supporting new hires through their first 90 days
  5. A scalable template system for lines of business, organisational pages and news

This created a consistent foundation for employee communications and self-service, while enabling different parts of the firm to meet their needs within one coherent experience.

Impact

Since its July 2023 launch, the intranet has become a central part of the employee experience.

261.2mPage views
74.5mProductivity-widget clicks
38mSearches initiated
10.2mNews article views
2.1mNew hires engaged
91%Understood available resources

Leadership takeaway: This was an enterprise transformation, not simply a platform redesign. It established the conditions for multidisciplinary delivery, created alignment among senior stakeholders and set a durable employee-centred product direction.
